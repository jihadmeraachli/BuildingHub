**LinkedIn (Tatawwor page), with the same image:**
```
One ledger line: $100.00 + LL 5,000,000 @ 89,500 = $155.87.

Every building in Lebanon runs on two currencies at once, and the rate moves. Generic property software either forces one currency or silently recalculates history when the rate changes, and a committee that can't reproduce last year's numbers can't defend them at the AGM.

Abniyah records both amounts and freezes the rate on the entry itself. Last year's books are last year's books.

One account covers a compound, its blocks and every unit; a unit's balance is the same figure from the block or from the compound. Org-level access separates roles: the concierge can handle issues and never touches money.

We're taking a small number of buildings and portfolios into beta.
```
