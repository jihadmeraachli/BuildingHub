## What I did not use, and why

**Ghost-cursor screen-demo clips (the new asset type) — held back on
purpose.** Week 1's brief, from the calendar and this task, is "say the
problem, do not sell." A screen recording of Finance → Book or Reports →
Unit statement, however smooth, is a product demo — that's Week 2's job
(`content-calendar.md` Posts 4–5: record-an-expense, dual-currency entry).
Using one here would blur the line the calendar draws on purpose between
"we understand your problem" (Week 1, earns saves/DMs) and "here's what we
built" (Week 2, earns trial interest). Recommendation: save the ghost-cursor
format for Week 2's batch, where a same-style shot list (page → action →
action, read-only, Tulip block, demo accounts can't write) will do more work
than it would here.

**Still screenshots of the demo** (`public/marketing/shot-*.jpg`) — same
reasoning: they show the product, which this week deliberately doesn't.
