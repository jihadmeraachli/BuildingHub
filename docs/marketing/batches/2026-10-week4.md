## The story: the mazout notebook

End of the month in a Beirut building. The natour (same man as the Rami film,
same chair by the entrance) goes door to door with a dog-eared notebook
collecting the generator money. Second floor: "I paid you last week." He
turns the pages; a coffee stain sits exactly where that line should be. The
committee head, at her kitchen table that night, calculator and three
notebooks, adding it up for the third time. The notebook is the ledger, the
memory, and the argument, all at once.

Answer (post 12): the natour records the payment in the app the moment it is
handed over; the resident's phone buzzes with the receipt; the statement
shows "Paid"; nobody turns pages.

## Post 10 · Monday 20 Oct, 7:30 PM · Reel + FB + TikTok — the problem (~35s)

Four Flow clips, same method as the Rami film (docs: week3 "How it was
made"). Transliterated dialogue only, Omni Flash for the spoken scenes, Veo
3.1 for the silent ones with a start frame for continuity. No children in
this one, so the child-safety filter is not in play. Avoid doors closing and
car entries. Use `squishy-natour-photo.jpg` as the natour's reference frame.

**Scene 1 (Omni, 8s, dialogue).** The natour, 60s, grey moustache, blue
work shirt, climbs an ordinary Beirut stairwell with a worn notebook and a
pen behind his ear. Knocks. A woman in her 40s opens, dish towel on her
shoulder. He says: "Marhaba madame, mazout el shaher." She: "Ya Abou Samir,
dafa3tellak el jem3a el madye!" He opens the notebook, frowns, turns a page.

**Scene 2 (Veo, 8s, silent).** Close on the notebook in his hands: pencil
columns, crossed-out numbers, a brown coffee ring sitting exactly over one
line. His thumb rubs the stain. Slow push in.

**Scene 3 (Omni, 8s, dialogue).** Same landing, a neighbour in a tracksuit
leans over the railing from the floor above: "Shou fi?" The woman: "Byeoul
ma dafa3et." Abou Samir, apologetic, closing the notebook: "Ma3leish, bshouf
el daftar el tene." Nobody is angry; everybody is tired.

**Scene 4 (Veo, 8s, silent).** Night. The committee head, 50s, reading
glasses, at a kitchen table under a warm lamp: three notebooks open, a
calculator, a cold cup of coffee. She rubs her eyes and starts again.
