## Post 7 · Monday 6 Oct, 7:30 PM Beirut (Tue 3:30 AM Sydney, AEDT) · Reel + FB + TikTok

**Asset:** `Downloads/Abniyah Week3 Visuals/post7-squishy-ai.mp4`, 43s, 1080×1920.
Jey's story: Rami (8) leaves his orange squishy cube on the lobby bench on the
way to football; the natour finds it and keeps it, not knowing whose it is;
two days later Rami searches his room; weeks later he spots it beside the
natour's chair at the entrance. Ends on the series outro "هالمشكلة، وغيرها
كتير. محلولة!" — the product (Lost & Found) is NOT shown, by Jey's choice; the
caption makes the connection. AI-disclosure card up front, AI label on TikTok.

**How it was made (so the next one is faster):** five Veo/Omni clips in
Google Flow. Lessons: (1) Arabic SCRIPT in a prompt trips the child-safety
filter; transliterated Lebanese ("Rami, yalla asra3, t'akhkharna!") passes and
is pronounced well by **Omni Flash**; Veo 3.1 handles the silent scenes and
takes a start FRAME (`squishy-1-last.png`) for lobby continuity, Omni does
not. (2) Describe a child minimally; the filter reacts to description density.
(3) Never animate a door closing or a car entry; cut around it. Stitch:
scratchpad `squishy-cards.mjs` + `squishy-xfade.mjs` (cross-fades, a 1s dip to
black before the natour scene, 2s fade-up).
